## Direction

The prompt locked the visual system to true black `#050505`, safety yellow `#FFD400`, and pure white; oversized condensed athletic type; screen-print and brush textures; angular real-photo crops; transparent official branding; generous readable spacing; and restrained motion cues. It explicitly prohibited blue, fake statistics, fake awards, invented partners, and baked-in UI text for the production site.

The production implementation intentionally keeps all real copy, navigation, buttons, forms, and accessibility labels code-native. Generated concepts are design references and are not served as page screenshots.
